# 📊 Tableau Direct Selling Business Analytics Dashboard

## Overview
An interactive Tableau dashboard built to analyze the performance of a direct-selling business using a synthetic dataset.

## Objectives
- Monitor sales performance
- Analyze distributor activity
- Evaluate sponsor performance
- Compare product categories
- Study the impact of promotional offers
- Analyze commission payouts
- Track team performance

## Dashboard KPIs
- Total Sales
- Total Commission
- Total Distributors
- Average Sales per Distributor

## Visualizations
- Monthly Sales Trend
- Active vs Inactive Members
- Sales by Rank
- Product Category Analysis
- Offer % Impact on Sales
- Sponsor Performance
- Team Performance
- State-wise Sales Analysis

## Tools Used
- Tableau


## Dataset
Synthetic dataset created for portfolio purposes to simulate a direct-selling business environment.

## Key Business Insights
- A small group of distributors contributes a significant share of total sales.
- Higher-ranked distributors consistently generate stronger revenue.
- Product category performance varies across the portfolio.
- Promotional offers show a measurable relationship with sales performance.
- Team leaders with larger, engaged networks drive higher sales.
- Regional performance differences highlight opportunities for targeted growth.
